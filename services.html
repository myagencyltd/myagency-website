How a project runs

Four stages, repeated every month.

1. Diagnose

We look at what's already running — accounts, content, past numbers — before proposing anything new.

2. Plan

A short roadmap with clear budgets, channels and the one or two numbers that define success.

3. Launch

Campaigns, content and funnels go live, with tracking in place from day one.

4. Report & refine

Monthly reporting against the agreed numbers, with changes made on the way, not just after.
